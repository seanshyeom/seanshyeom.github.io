Seungho (Sean) Yeom

Hi, I'm Seungho (Sean) Yeom, a visiting undergraduate researcher in the Advanced Robotics and Controls Lab (ARCLab) at UC San Diego, advised by Prof. Michael Yip, and a mechanical engineering undergraduate at Yonsei University.
I work on compliant whole-body control for humanoid loco-manipulation and on policies and world action models with memory for long-horizon tasks under partial observability. I'm interested in using foundation models, especially agentic AI that uses vision-language models (VLMs) to direct a whole-body controller, to help humanoids carry out long-horizon, fine-grained tasks through compliant loco-manipulation.
Previously, I was an undergraduate researcher in the Machine Learning and Control Systems (MLCS) Lab at Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Jongeun Choi.

Projects

Re-ACT on Sensing: Force-Aware Action Chunking with Responsive Temporal Ensemble for Robot Manipulation
Project lead, graduate course (Machine Learning and Programming), Yonsei University
paper / code

Inspired by how quickly people react when pushed, Re-ACT re-weights the temporal ensemble of Action Chunking with Transformers toward recent action chunks when a force-torque sensor detects an external force. Validated on the Rainbow Robotics RB-Y1 mobile bimanual manipulator.

A-You (Assist-You Robot): A Wheelchair-Mounted Bimanual Manipulator
Project lead, graduate course (Human Robotics), Yonsei University

A foldable dual-arm robot mounted on a wheelchair that helps people with limited mobility perform everyday tasks on their own. I designed the 14-DOF robot, built its URDF, and controlled it in PyBullet.

Impedance-Controlled Gait Assistance Robot for Children with Poliomyelitis
MLCS Lab, Yonsei University

A gait-assistive robot that moves compliantly with the user to provide gentle, adaptive support. I contributed to the hardware design and validated the impedance control strategy.
